what this page is, under the renewed design2026-10-11 — switched on

Per-NIC balancing of proxied traffic belongs to HypoMux, not mihomo. HypoMux opens each upstream socket from the adapter it picks (mihomo only dials its SOCKS5 endpoint at 127.0.0.1:10800), and this page cannot read HypoMux's per-NIC split: it lives in the GUI's engine — a child of hypomux.exe driven over its stdio channel — and there is no control API on 10800/10801. The dashboard's /api/status does carry HypoMux's total (carrying / carried_conns).

So everything below is mihomo's accounting: its own direct connections plus whatever it dials itself. The previous design's per-NIC outbounds and rings (HY2LB, DIRECT-LB, MIX, generated by the adapter watcher) are retired — on 2026-10-11 the switch ran for real: they are gone from runtime\config.yaml, the watcher is stopped (task Disabled, zero Watch-Adapters processes), and a re-run of hypomux-distribution.ps1 left the config byte-identical. Legacy ring names are therefore absent from the cards below — that absence is the switch, not a fault.

Every proxied dial now follows the renewed design, free and paid alike: AUTO (mihomo picks the upstream — since the fourth change a 911-first 4:1 mix, four filter:-selected 911 exits against one aliyun-native) leaves through the HypoMux hop, and the paid provider carries override: dialer-proxy: hypomux — so neither credits an adapter in the tables below. That absence is the design, not a gap in the data. And since the third revision later on 2026-10-11 HypoMux is a hop for everything: the catch-all is MATCH,DISPATCH, so unlisted traffic leaves through DISPATCH = fallback [hypomux, DIRECT] — not proxied, but dialled by HypoMux all the same, so it does not credit an adapter here either. Only loopback, the private ranges — and, by owner order since 2026-10-12, the two DeepSeek rules — stay raw DIRECT and can appear as mihomo's own.

Kept as that revision's record: under the earlier MATCH,DIRECT shape (second revision, earlier the same day) unlisted traffic never entered mihomo's proxy chains at all, so it followed the OS route and could not appear here as proxied. DISPATCH is a fallback whose second member is DIRECT: with HypoMux's engine down the group degrades to that OS route, which is what keeps the machine online.

notes

Rates are computed in this page from mihomo's /connections byte counters — no collector needed.
A member shown down but carrying bytes means its health probe disagrees with reality; check its url/expected-status.
Adapters with no outbound mapping (e.g. hotspot clients) cannot appear here: ICS forwards their traffic outside mihomo.
HypoMux's own per-NIC split is not on this page and cannot be fetched from it — the numbers here are mihomo's own accounting (direct traffic and the dials mihomo makes itself), and for the AUTO mix a dial that leaves by an adapter HypoMux chose. Since the third revision that is also true of every dispatched direct connection (DISPATCH), so almost nothing on this page credits an adapter any more — that is the design. The dashboard's /api/status reports HypoMux's carried total (carrying, carried_conns); the per-NIC split itself is only in the GUI.
Reads http://127.0.0.1:9097 directly (mihomo allows cross-origin reads), which is why this page works even while the dashboard server is unchanged. Since the 2026-10-11 switch the config is the renewed design outright: AUTO (mihomo's upstream pick, dialled through HypoMux) is what the rule-named set resolves to, and since the fourth change that group is a 911-first 4:1 mix — load-balance + round-robin, four 911 exits chosen by filter: (香港|HK2022|AT 80 ms · 台湾|TW2035|AT 112 ms · 新加坡|SG2034|AT 115 ms · 马来西亚|MY2032|AT 118 ms) against one aliyun-native, so 4 of every 5 proxied connections spend the metered plan, measured 4:1 over 20 connections. It was fallback [HYPOMUX-PATH] + use: [clashmi] (free first) before that, and HYPOMUX-PATH is now defined but unreferenced — it reads unhealthy through the hop (hy2-upstream is QUIC), which is why the mix names aliyun-native directly. LOCAL is select [DISPATCH, DIRECT] (it was select [DIRECT] until the third revision), and BENCH lists only live names. The catch-all became MATCH,DIRECT earlier the same day (owner) and then MATCH,DISPATCH (third revision: "the hypomux dispatch all the traffics to the NICs") — the proxy set is our rules, the learner's escalations and the 911 subscription's own list (RULE-SET,clashmi-911,AUTO, 354 entries), and everything else is NIC-dispatched by HypoMux through DISPATCH = fallback [hypomux, DIRECT] instead of following the OS route. Only loopback, the private ranges and the two DeepSeek rules still go raw DIRECT. The SQX data path is the one proxied set that does not use the mix: dukascopy.com moved LOCAL → DATA in the same fourth change, and DATA is a round-robin over a few stable exits (filter: "香港|新加坡|日本|德国"). The previous design's HY2LB / DIRECT-LB / MIX rings and per-NIC outbounds are retired — gone from the config and no longer written, because the watcher that generated them is stopped (hypomux-distribution.ps1 did it, and does not start or stop HypoMux itself). Per-NIC balance of proxied traffic is HypoMux's job and is not visible here — and since the third revision that is true of direct traffic too.
